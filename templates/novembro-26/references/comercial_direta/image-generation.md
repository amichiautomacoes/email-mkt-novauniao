## Prompt utilizado

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: a wide hero image for an HTML marketing email for Brazilian label supplier Nova União Etiquetas. Create a polished photorealistic illustrative studio still life of the actual product categories: two white adhesive-label rolls with visible kraft cardboard cores and a few blank white labels peeling gracefully from the rolls; three premium white rectangular clothing hang tags with small holes and thin dark strings resting on a neatly folded navy-blue fabric swatch; two compact glossy black thermal-transfer ribbon rolls. Palette: nearly black navy #010A1B background, rich institutional blue #2967A2 and controlled cobalt #1C64CB rim light, neutral white products, subtle warm wood work surface. Horizontal landscape composition around 2:1, all products clearly within frame and grouped near center so the arrangement still reads at phone width. Close three-quarter product camera, tactile paper edges and fabric, refined commercial product lighting, realistic object geometry, quiet deep navy backdrop, strong material detail, sophisticated restrained styling. This is a demonstrative product mockup, not documentation of a real customer or guaranteed product specification. Absolutely no words, letters, numbers, price tags, logos, brand names, watermarks, humans, infographic icons or interface graphics. No promotional copy embedded in the image. The email supplies all copy as separate HTML text.
